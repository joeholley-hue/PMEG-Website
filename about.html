About Paragon Medical Education Group

Delivering Premium EMS Education Since Our Founding

Our Mission

We believe hands-on experience and one-on-one mentorship are the best teachers. We bring premium EMS education to you, in any location and under any conditions, with courses specifically customized to meet your goals.

EMS Education Excellence

Paragon Medical Education Group provides comprehensive EMS education covering a wide range of healthcare disciplines including:

  • Emergency Medical Services Training
  • Mass Casualty Management
  • Trauma Care & Advanced Life Support
  • Healthcare Professional Development
  • Specialized Medical Disciplines

What Sets Us Apart

Award-winning faculty mentors with real-world experience

World-renowned Cadaver training model for practical, hands-on learning

Real-world scenarios that prepare you for actual field conditions

Customized programs tailored to your specific goals and needs

Our Commitment

At Paragon Medical Education Group, we are committed to excellence in every aspect of medical education. Our faculty, curriculum, and training methods are designed to produce healthcare professionals who are confident, competent, and ready to make a difference in emergency medical services.

Leadership & Expertise

Our team consists of veteran EMS professionals, trauma surgeons, and medical educators with decades of combined experience in the field. We've trained thousands of healthcare professionals who are now leading EMS agencies and hospitals across the nation.
