Our Classes & Training Programs

Comprehensive Education Across Multiple Disciplines

Available Training Programs

Paragon Medical Education Group offers a comprehensive selection of classes and training programs designed for healthcare professionals and EMS personnel. All programs feature hands-on instruction, expert faculty, and real-world scenarios.

Core Training Categories

EMS Fundamentals

Foundation-level training for emergency medical services personnel covering essential principles and practices.

Advanced Life Support

Advanced training for certified EMS professionals in critical care, advanced interventions, and complex patient management.

Trauma Specialty

Specialized trauma care training with hands-on Cadaver training model for realistic preparation and competency development.

Mass Casualty Incident Response

Training in coordinating and managing large-scale emergency incidents with multiple casualties and complex logistics.

Medical Specialty Training

Specialized training in specific healthcare disciplines tailored to your professional needs and career goals.

Leadership & Mentorship

Professional development programs for EMS leaders and instructors to enhance teaching and management skills.

Program Features

Hands-On Instruction: Practical training with real-world scenarios and our world-renowned Cadaver training model

Expert Faculty: Award-winning faculty mentors with extensive field experience

Customized Content: Programs tailored to your specific learning goals and organizational needs

Flexible Delivery: On-site training at your location or at our facilities

Continuing Education: Programs designed to advance your career and maintain professional certification
